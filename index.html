Computer vision, shipped to air

Real-time systems running on live sports broadcasts. A wrong call goes out to a national audience — so reliability is the whole game.

// the path to air 01 / 05

live TV 5–10 s to air

Seven broadcast cameras feed the system at 240 frames per second over an SRT link.

GStreamer ingests every stream with GPU-optimized reads — nothing dropped on the way in.

A Triton-hosted model zoo — RF-DETR, TrackNetV5, SAM — tracks the ball and pinpoints the bounce.

The verdict — in or out, and how close to the line — is rendered as a broadcast graphic in Godot.

The call hits the national broadcast within 5–10 seconds of the bounce.
