Academic Focus

I am a passionate Machine Learning Engineer and Software Developer currently pursuing my Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder (GPA: 3.93/4.00). My professional experience includes ML engineering internships at Owl AI (building real-time snowboard analytics with TensorRT and MediaMTX) and Idaho National Laboratory (developing segmentation models for nuclear materials using SAM/SAM2 and HPC infrastructure). Currently, I serve as a Teaching Assistant for Distributed Systems at CU Boulder, supporting Prof. Mark Zhao. I'm also actively involved in hackathons, winning 3rd place in T9-MediHack 2025 and 7th place in AWS Jam, and contribute as a Technical/Data Consultant at Data Buffs and Co-Founder of Layers Club.
